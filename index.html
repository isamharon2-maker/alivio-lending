Frequently Asked Questions

Before You Apply

How do I submit an application?

Click any Apply Online or Fill Out the Form button to open the Alivio Lending application form.

How long does processing take?

The target processing time is 24–72 hours after a complete application is received. Verification or other requirements may affect the actual timeframe.

Does submitting the form guarantee approval?

No. Every application is subject to eligibility requirements, verification, and the lender's decision.

What should I check before accepting a loan?

Review the amount you will receive, interest rate, all fees, total repayment amount, due dates, late payment consequences, and the full agreement.
